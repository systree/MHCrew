## Introduction

Mover Hero Crew is a mobile app for moving companies. It connects your crew in the field with the jobs managed in your business system (GoHighLevel). Crew members use it to see their jobs and update progress. Admins use it to manage the team, configure billing, and keep an eye on what is happening in the field.

**There are two roles:**

- **Crew** — movers who view and work through their assigned jobs
- **Admin** — managers who configure the app, manage team members, and oversee all jobs

**How to access the app:**

The app runs in a web browser — you do not need to download anything from an app store. Open the app URL in your browser on your phone or computer. On a phone, you can save it to your home screen so it works like a regular app.

**Installing to your home screen (recommended for phones):**

On iPhone:
1. Open the app URL in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen."
4. Tap "Add" to confirm.

On Android:
1. Open the app URL in Chrome.
2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top right.
3. Tap "Add to Home Screen."
4. Tap "Add" to confirm.

Once installed, the app opens full-screen just like a native app. You can use it offline — any actions you take will sync automatically when you reconnect to the internet.

## Getting Started

### Logging In

The app uses your mobile number for sign-in. You do not need a password on first login.

**First time logging in:**

1. Open the app. You will see the sign-in screen.
2. Enter your Australian mobile number (for example: 0412 345 678). The app will format it automatically.
3. Tap "Send Code." A 6-digit code will be sent to your phone by text message.
4. Enter the 6-digit code. The boxes will move forward automatically as you type. You can also paste the code if it appears in a banner above your keyboard.
5. You will be prompted to create a 4-digit PIN. Enter a PIN of your choice.
6. Re-enter the same PIN to confirm it.
7. You are now logged in.

**Returning logins:**

1. Enter your mobile number and tap "Send Code."
2. Enter the 6-digit code from your text message.
3. Enter your 4-digit PIN to complete the sign-in.

If you forget your PIN, tap "Use OTP instead" on the PIN screen. This takes you back to the code entry step, and after entering the correct code you will be asked to create a new PIN.

**If you are logged out:**

The app will return you to the sign-in screen. Simply log in again using the steps above.

## For Crew Members

After logging in, you land on the Job List. A navigation bar at the bottom of the screen lets you switch between your jobs, and access your profile.

---

### Job List (My Jobs)

The job list shows all jobs assigned to you. At the top, two tabs let you switch between views:

- **Upcoming** — jobs that are currently active or not yet started
- **History** — completed and cancelled jobs

Upcoming jobs are grouped by date so you can see what is on for today, tomorrow, and the days ahead.

Each job card shows:
- The customer's name
- The pickup address
- The scheduled date and time
- The current status (shown as a coloured badge)

Tap any job card to open the full job detail.

To refresh your job list manually, tap the refresh icon in the top right corner of the screen.

**What the status badges mean:**

| Badge | What it means |
|-------|--------------|
| Assigned | You have been assigned this job. It has not started yet. |
| En Route | You are on your way to the pickup address. |
| Arrived | You are at the pickup location. |
| In Progress | The job is actively underway. The clock is running. |
| Completed | The job is finished. |
| Cancelled | The job was cancelled. |

---

### Job Detail

Tap a job to see its full details. This screen is your main working view while on a job.

**Information shown:**

- **Customer** — the customer's name and phone number. Tap the phone number to call them directly.
- **Scheduled** — the date and time the job is booked for
- **Estimated value** — the quoted value of the job, if set
- **Locations** — the pickup address and (if applicable) the drop-off address. Navigation buttons let you open Google Maps directions to either location with one tap.
- **Items** — a summary of what is being moved, if entered in the system
- **Crew Notes** — any notes left for the crew about this job
- **Job Type** — shown as a badge in the header: Door to Door, Depot to Depot, or Quote (if billing rules are configured by your admin)

**Moving a job through its stages:**

The large button at the top of the screen tells you what action is available next. Tap it to progress the job.

```
Assigned → Start Driving → En Route → I've Arrived → Arrived → Start Job → In Progress → Complete Job → Completed
```

Each step requires a confirmation before it saves. This prevents accidental taps.

When you tap "Start Job" to move to In Progress, a clock-in will happen automatically and time tracking will start. When you tap "Complete Job," you will be able to add optional completion notes, and the time tracker will stop automatically.

**Cancelling a job:**

A "Cancel Job" button appears at the bottom of the screen on any active job. Tapping it will ask you for a reason, which is required before the cancellation can be confirmed.

**Navigation shortcuts:**

- "To Pickup" button — opens Google Maps directions from your current location to the pickup address
- "To Drop-off" button — opens Google Maps directions from the pickup address to the drop-off address

### Time Tracking

The time tracker appears on the job detail screen once you are at the job site (when the job is in "Arrived," "In Progress," or "Completed" status).

**When you tap "Start Job"**, time tracking starts automatically. You do not need to do anything manually.

**When you tap "Complete Job"**, time tracking stops automatically.

If you need to take a break during a job:

1. Tap "Start Break" in the time tracker section.
2. When you are ready to resume, tap "End Break."
3. Break time is tracked separately and excluded from your total working time.

The time tracker shows:
- How long the job has been in progress
- Break time (if on a break)
- A summary of total time and break time once you clock out

Your timesheet records are used when creating invoices — if your admin has set up billing rules, the hours will be pre-filled automatically.

### Photos

A photo section appears on the job detail screen for any job that has not been cancelled.

**To take a photo:**

1. Scroll down to the Photos section on the job detail screen.
2. Choose a photo type: Before, After, Damage, Item, or Other.
3. Tap "Take Photo" to use your camera, or "Choose from Gallery" to select an existing photo.
4. After selecting, tap "Upload" to attach it to the job.

**To view photos:**

Tap any photo thumbnail to open it full size.

**To delete a photo:**

Tap the photo to open it, then tap the delete button. You will be asked to confirm before it is removed.

Photos are stored against the job and can be viewed by admins.

### Invoices

You can create and send invoices directly from a job. Invoices go to the customer through the business system (GoHighLevel) automatically.

**Viewing invoices on a job:**

Open the job detail. If any invoices have been created for this job, they will appear in the Invoices section. Tap an invoice to expand it and see the line items and amount.

**Invoice status meanings:**

| Status | What it means |
|--------|--------------|
| Draft | Created but not yet sent to the customer |
| Sent | Sent to the customer and awaiting payment |
| Viewed | The customer has opened the invoice |
| Part Paid | A partial payment has been recorded |
| Paid | Fully paid |

**Creating an invoice:**

1. Open the job detail for the job you want to invoice.
2. Tap "Create Invoice."
3. The invoice title is pre-filled with the customer's name. You can change it.
4. Fill in the line items — each item needs a description, quantity, and unit price.
   - Tap "+ Add Item" to add more items.
   - Tap the × button to remove an item.
5. Set an optional due date.
6. Tap "Send Invoice" to create and send it.

The invoice is created in GoHighLevel and immediately sent to the customer by SMS and email. You do not need to take any further action. If the send fails for any reason, the invoice will remain as a draft and a "Send Invoice to Client" button will appear on the invoice so you can retry.

**Billing rules (if enabled by your admin):**

If your admin has enabled billing rules, the invoice screen will show a helper section based on the job type:

- **Door to Door** — enter the hours worked and the hourly rate. The app will automatically calculate the total and add a callout charge on top.
- **Depot to Depot** — enter hours and hourly rate. No callout charge is added.
- **Quote** — if there is an accepted estimate, you will see a shortcut to create the invoice from the estimate instead of filling it in manually.

**If an invoice is still in Draft (send failed):**

Expand the invoice in the Invoices section on the job detail page. If it shows "Draft" status, tap "Send Invoice to Client" to send it manually.

**Marking an invoice as paid:**

Expand the invoice in the Invoices section. If the invoice has been sent, you will see a "Mark as Paid" button. Tap it and confirm to record the payment.

**Deleting a draft invoice:**

Expand the draft invoice and tap "Delete Draft." You will be asked to confirm. Only drafts can be deleted — once sent, an invoice cannot be deleted from the app.

### Estimates

Estimates are quotes created in GoHighLevel before the job is confirmed. If your admin has turned on the "Show Estimates to Crew" setting, you can view and act on estimates directly from the job detail screen.

**To view estimates:**

1. Open the job detail.
2. Tap "Show Estimates" — the number of estimates will be shown in brackets if any exist.
3. Tap an estimate to expand it and see the items and total.

**Estimate status meanings:**

| Status | What it means |
|--------|--------------|
| Draft | Not yet sent to the customer |
| Sent | Sent to the customer for review |
| Accepted | The customer has accepted it |
| Declined | The customer has declined it |
| Expired | The estimate has passed its expiry date |
| Invoiced | An invoice has already been created from this estimate |

**Converting an estimate to an invoice:**

If an estimate has been accepted by the customer, you can convert it to an invoice:

1. Expand the estimate in the Estimates section.
2. Tap "Convert to Invoice."
3. The invoice is created and sent to the customer automatically.

Alternatively, when you tap "Create Invoice" from the job detail, if there are any accepted estimates you will see a shortcut at the top to create the invoice from that estimate directly.

### Profile

Tap the Profile icon in the bottom navigation bar to open your profile page.

**What you can do here:**

- **View your contact details** — your name, mobile number, and email
- **See your account info** — when you joined and your authentication method
- **Manage notifications** — turn push notifications on or off. If you have never enabled them, the app may prompt you automatically when you visit this screen.
- **Sign out** — tap "Sign Out" at the bottom of the page. A confirmation will appear before you are logged out.

**Push notifications:**

The Notifications section shows whether job push notifications are currently enabled on your device. Tap "Enable" to turn them on, or "Disable" to turn them off.

If notifications show as "Blocked," your browser has denied permission. To fix this, you need to allow notifications for this site in your browser or phone settings.

## For Admins

Admins see all the same screens as crew members, plus an Admin Panel accessible from the bottom navigation bar. The Admin Panel is only visible to users with the Admin role.

---

### Admin Dashboard

The Admin Dashboard is your control centre. It has two sections:

**Configuration** — links to each admin settings area:
- Pipeline Setup
- Stage Mapping
- Crew Management
- Invoice Settings
- Jobs
- Notification Settings
- Driver Locations
- Billing Rules

**Quick Actions** — buttons to run maintenance tasks:
- **Sync Location** — re-fetches your business name and timezone from GoHighLevel
- **Initialize Required Fields** — creates the 7 required custom fields in GoHighLevel that the app needs. Run this once during initial setup.
- **Sync Crew** — re-imports all GoHighLevel users as crew members in the app
- **Sync Jobs** — pulls the latest jobs from GoHighLevel into the app
- **Sync Pipeline Stages** — re-fetches the pipeline stages from GoHighLevel
- **Refresh Field Definitions** — re-imports the custom field schema from GoHighLevel

A collapsible "Setup & recovery guide" panel explains what to do after reinstalling the app or after a data reset.

---

### Jobs

The Jobs section shows all jobs across all crew members — not just yours.

**To access it:** From the Admin Dashboard, tap "Jobs."

**What you see:**

- A total count of jobs at the top
- Filter tabs across the top to filter by status: All, Assigned, En Route, Arrived, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled. Each tab shows a count.
- Job cards showing: customer name, assigned crew, scheduled date and time, pickup address, status badge, job type, and estimated value

**To view the estimates for a job:**

Tap any job card to expand it. If there are estimates for that job in GoHighLevel, they will appear in an Estimates section. You can see the estimate details including line items, total, status, and expiry date.

**Note:** Admins view jobs here in read-only mode. To progress a job's status, crew members use the job detail screen in the regular crew view.

---

### Crew Management

This section shows all crew members registered for your location.

**To access it:** From the Admin Dashboard, tap "Crew Management."

**What you see:**

- Filter tabs: All, Active, Inactive — with counts
- Each crew member's name, role (Crew or Admin), phone number, and active status

**What you can do:**

- **Toggle active/inactive** — use the Active toggle on a crew member's card to enable or disable their access to the app. Inactive crew members cannot log in.
- **Change role** — switch a crew member between "Crew" and "Admin" using the role selector. Admins have access to the Admin Panel.

Changes save immediately. A brief success or error message confirms the outcome.

**Syncing crew from GoHighLevel:**

If a new staff member has been added in GoHighLevel but does not appear in the crew list, go to the Admin Dashboard and tap "Sync Crew." This re-imports all GoHighLevel users.

### Invoice Settings

Configure how invoices are created from the app.

**To access it:** From the Admin Dashboard, tap "Invoice Settings."

**Settings available:**

**Enable Tax on Invoices** — when turned on, a tax line is added to every invoice created from the app. When you enable it, you will see additional fields:
- **Tax Name** — what to call the tax on the invoice (e.g. GST, Sales Tax, VAT)
- **Tax Rate (%)** — the percentage to apply (e.g. 10 for 10%)
- **Calculation** — whether the tax is added on top of item prices (Exclusive) or already included in item prices (Inclusive)

**Show Estimates to Crew** — when turned on, crew members can see the Estimates section on their job detail screen. When turned off, estimates are hidden from crew.

Tap "Save Settings" when done.

---

### Billing Rules

Billing Rules add intelligent helpers to the invoice creation screen, based on the type of job.

**To access it:** From the Admin Dashboard, tap "Billing Rules."

**Settings available:**

**Enable Billing Rules** — the master switch. When turned off, invoice creation works as it always has. When turned on, crew members see billing helpers based on the job type.

**Door-to-Door Callout (minutes)** — when billing rules are enabled and the job type is "Door to Door," a callout charge is automatically added to invoices. This setting controls how many minutes to charge. The default is 30 minutes. For example, at an hourly rate of $220, a 30-minute callout charge would add $110 to the invoice.

**Partial Payments (Interstate)** — when enabled, a 1/3–1/3–1/3 payment schedule is added to new invoices for large jobs.

Tap "Save" when done.

**How billing rules affect invoice creation:**

When a crew member taps "Create Invoice" on a job:

- If the job type is **Door to Door**: the crew enters hours worked and hourly rate. The app calculates the total and automatically adds the callout charge as a separate line item.
- If the job type is **Depot to Depot**: the crew enters hours worked and hourly rate. No callout charge is added.
- If the job type is **Quote**: if there is an accepted estimate, the crew sees a shortcut to create the invoice from that estimate. Otherwise, they fill in the invoice manually.

### Notification Settings

Control which push notifications are sent to crew and admins.

**To access it:** From the Admin Dashboard, tap "Notification Settings."

**Crew Notifications:**

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| Job Assigned | Sends a push notification to the crew member when a job is assigned to them |

**Admin Notifications:**

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| Status Changed | Notifies admins when a crew member updates a job status |
| Invoice Created | Notifies admins when a crew member creates an invoice |
| Invoice Sent | Notifies admins when a crew member sends an invoice to a client |
| Invoice Deleted | Notifies admins when a crew member deletes a draft invoice |

Tap "Save Settings" when done.

Note: Notifications are only delivered to users who have enabled them on their device. Crew members must opt in through the Profile screen.

### Driver Locations (Crew Map)

The Crew Map shows where your active drivers are located, based on where they were when they last updated their job status.

**To access it:** From the Admin Dashboard, tap "Driver Locations."

**What you see:**

An interactive map with a coloured marker for each active driver. Each marker shows the driver's initials. Tap a marker to see a popup with:
- The driver's name
- The customer's name
- The current job status
- How long ago the location was captured

**Colour coding:**

| Colour | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Blue | En Route |
| Yellow | Arrived |
| Green | In Progress |

**Important:** The map shows where a driver was at the time they last changed their job status — it is not live GPS tracking. Locations are only captured when a crew member taps a status button (such as "Start Driving" or "Start Job").

The map refreshes automatically every 60 seconds.

## Notifications

The app can send push notifications to your phone, even when the app is not open, as long as you have notifications enabled.

**What triggers a notification:**

For crew members:
- When a new job is assigned to you

For admins:
- When a crew member updates a job status
- When a crew member creates an invoice
- When a crew member sends an invoice to a client
- When a crew member deletes a draft invoice

These can each be turned on or off by an admin in the Notification Settings screen.

**How to allow notifications:**

When you first visit the app, or when you open the Profile screen, the app will ask if you want to enable notifications. Tap "Allow" when your browser shows the permission prompt. If you miss it, go to your Profile and tap the "Enable" button in the Notifications section.

If your browser says notifications are "Blocked," you need to allow them in your browser or phone settings:
- On iPhone (Safari): Settings → Safari → Notifications → find the app URL and allow it
- On Android (Chrome): tap the lock icon in the address bar → Notifications → Allow

## Frequently Asked Questions

**I did not receive my login code — what do I do?**

Wait up to 60 seconds. If it has not arrived, tap "Resend code" on the code entry screen. Make sure you entered your number correctly. The code is only valid for a short time.

**Can I use the app on a computer?**

Yes. The app works in any modern web browser on a desktop or laptop. The experience is optimised for mobile phones, but all features work on a computer.

**What does each job status mean?**

- **Assigned** — the job has been booked and assigned to you, but you have not started driving yet
- **En Route** — you are on your way to the job
- **Arrived** — you are at the pickup location
- **In Progress** — the job is actively underway and time is being tracked
- **Completed** — the job is done
- **Cancelled** — the job has been called off

**Who receives the invoice when it is sent?**

The invoice is sent to the customer via GoHighLevel (the business management system your company uses). GoHighLevel handles the delivery — typically by email — based on the customer's contact details in the system.

**Can I edit an invoice after it is sent?**

No. Once an invoice has been sent to the customer, it cannot be edited or deleted from the app. If you need to make a change, contact your admin, who can make adjustments in GoHighLevel directly.

**Can I delete an invoice?**

You can only delete draft invoices — invoices that have been created but not yet sent. Open the invoice in the Invoices section on the job detail page, expand it, and tap "Delete Draft." Once sent, an invoice cannot be deleted from the app.

**What is an estimate and how is it different from an invoice?**

An estimate is a quote sent to a customer before the job is confirmed. It shows what the job is expected to cost. An invoice is the final billing document issued after the job is complete (or at any time during the job). Estimates are created in GoHighLevel, not in the crew app. The crew app can display estimates (if enabled by your admin) and allows you to convert an accepted estimate into an invoice.

**I am not seeing the Estimates section on a job — why?**

The estimates feature must be turned on by an admin. Go to Admin Dashboard → Invoice Settings and enable "Show Estimates to Crew." If you are a crew member and cannot see it, ask your admin to check this setting.

**I am not seeing the Billing Rules helper when creating an invoice — why?**

Billing Rules must be enabled by an admin in Admin Dashboard → Billing Rules. Additionally, the job must have a job type assigned (Door to Door, Depot to Depot, or Quote) in GoHighLevel. If both conditions are met, the billing helper will appear when you create an invoice.

**The app says I am offline — what happens to my actions?**

Any actions you take while offline (such as updating a job status) are queued and will be sent automatically as soon as your internet connection is restored. You will see a banner at the top of the screen indicating you are offline.

**How do I update my PIN?**

Your PIN cannot be reset from within the Profile screen directly. To change your PIN, sign out, then log in again with your phone number and OTP code — you will be prompted to create a new PIN as part of the sign-in process.

**A new version of the app is available — what should I do?**

When a new version is available, a banner will appear at the top of the app saying an update is ready. Tap the "Update" button in the banner to load the new version. You will not lose any unsaved work — the app will refresh with the latest changes.
